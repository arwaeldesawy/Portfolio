Medical AI & systems

MediVault

Patient Medical History System with Fingerprint Identification

Overview

Full-stack medical platform integrating AI-based medical image analysis with secure electronic patient records.

Results & achievements

  • Integrated four medical vision models in one platform.
  • Implemented fingerprint-based patient identification.
  • Connected patient records with a Gemini chatbot and real-time communication.

Project stack

  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL
  • SQLAlchemy
  • PyTorch
  • TensorFlow/Keras
  • Gemini
  • WebSockets

Technical approach

Identify & retrieve

Fingerprint-based emergency identification connects a patient to their electronic medical history. FastAPI REST APIs manage patient data through PostgreSQL and SQLAlchemy.

Analyze medical images

Four deep learning models support brain tumor, pneumonia, bone fracture, and stroke detection, using PyTorch and TensorFlow/Keras.

Make information accessible

Google Gemini powers a medical chatbot and document simplification. WebSockets provide real-time communication.

Project highlights

  • Full-stack medical platform integrating AI-based medical image analysis with secure electronic patient records.
  • Integrated four deep learning models for brain tumor, pneumonia, bone fracture, and stroke detection.
  • Built FastAPI REST APIs with PostgreSQL and SQLAlchemy for secure, efficient patient data management.
  • Integrated Google Gemini for an AI-powered medical chatbot and document simplification, with WebSocket-based real-time communication.
  • Implemented fingerprint-based emergency patient identification.
